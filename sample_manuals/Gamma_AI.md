# Tutorial: Gamma AI Presentation Creator Masterclass - Step-by-Step Guide

### 1. Account Setup and Workspace Creation
Begin by signing up for an account and creating your dedicated workspace within Gamma AI  **[1]**. During the sign-up process, you will be guided through an initial configuration to tailor the experience to your needs  **[2]**. Setting up a workspace allows you to organize your projects and collaborate effectively from the start  **[3]**.

### 2. Selecting Your Creation Method
Gamma offers three primary ways to start a project: 'Paste in text', 'Generate', or 'Import'  **[4]**. For a quick start, the 'Prompt-to-presentation' method allows you to describe your idea and let the AI build the foundation  **[5]**. You can also decide if your output should be a standard presentation, a web page, or a document  **[6]**.

### 3. Strategic Research and Narrative Planning
Before generating slides, conduct deep market research using AI reasoning models or frameworks like Porter's Five Forces to ensure your content is high-quality  **[7]**. Transform your research findings into a compelling narrative story using the SEQA framework  **[8]**. This ensures the AI acts as a visualization layer for your specific insights rather than just generating generic text  **[9]**.

### 4. Defining the Outline and Slide Count
Enter your notes or a specific prompt to guide the AI's content generation  **[10]**  **[11]**. Review the AI-generated outline carefully and make manual adjustments to the structure before proceeding  **[12]**. At this stage, you can also manage your credits by selecting the exact number of cards, or slides, you want the AI to create  **[13]**.

### 5. Configuring Content and Audience Settings
Fine-tune how the AI handles your input by choosing to generate new content, condense existing notes, or preserve your original text  **[14]**. You should also specify the content depth, target audience, tone, and language to match your professional requirements  **[15]**. Additionally, customize your image sourcing preferences and slide dimensions for the best visual impact  **[16]**.

### 6. Choosing Themes and Visual Style
Select a design theme that aligns with your brand's aesthetic from Gamma's diverse library  **[17]**  **[18]**. If you have an existing brand identity, you can import custom themes directly from PowerPoint or Google Slides  **[19]**. Once your theme is selected, the AI will generate the initial presentation layout based on your previous configurations  **[20]**.

### 7. Refining Content with AI Editing Tools
Use the 'Edit with AI' tool to instantly adjust slide layouts or rewrite specific sections  **[21]**. You can command the AI to shorten, lengthen, or improve the text to make it more impactful  **[22]**. For a more professional touch, use AI to refine your slide titles into insight-led headlines that grab attention  **[23]**.

### 8. Manual Customization and Image Editing
Easily rearrange your slides using the film strip and list views for better logical flow  **[24]**. You can manually edit text and customize card styling, including colors and bleed settings, for a bespoke look  **[25]**. For visuals, use the built-in tools to set image focus points, remove backgrounds, or search the web for new high-quality photos  **[26]**  **[27]**.

### 9. Integrating Interactive Elements and Widgets
Enhance your cards by using the forward slash (/) command to insert content blocks or data-driven slides  **[28]**  **[29]**. You can integrate third-party apps such as YouTube, Loom, or Figma to make your presentation interactive  **[30]**. Use the Insert Widget menu to add more complex elements like charts, diagrams, and buttons  **[31]**  **[32]**.

### 10. Exporting and Delivering Your Presentation
Preview your final work in full-screen mode to ensure a seamless experience  **[33]**  **[34]**. When ready, export your presentation as a PDF, PowerPoint file, or PNG image for offline use  **[35]**  **[36]**. During live delivery, utilize the Spotlight feature for focused delivery and check the analytics menu to see how your audience engaged with the content  **[37]**  **[38]**.
